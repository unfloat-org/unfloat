# Community rules

1. Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people.
2. No medical advice, even from experts. Ask your eye doctor about your eyes.
3. Nothing for sale: no products, supplements, clinics or referral links.
4. Don't post medical records, scans or other health data. We don't collect it.
5. Share sources: post the link and say if you read the full paper or just the abstract.
6. Share only public professional information about doctors or researchers. Nobody is named as a partner without their OK.
7. What you share may be published, with credit.

Sudden new floaters, flashes, or a shadow or curtain over your vision need a same-day eye exam.

Something wrong? Tell a moderator on Discord or message Daniil.
