# Community rules

These rules apply here and on the unfloat Discord.

- Be kind. Disagree with ideas, not people. Harassment and condescending replies aren't welcome.
- Don't give medical advice, even if you work in medicine. For questions about your own eyes, please ask your eye doctor. Don't promise a cure or a timeline.
- No selling or promotion of products, supplements or clinics, and no referral links.
- Don't post your own or anyone else's medical records, scans or personal health data. You can talk about living with floaters on Discord, but we don't collect those stories as data. Don't start health surveys or symptom spreadsheets.
- Link the source when you make a scientific claim. Say whether you've read the full paper or just the abstract, and separate what it found from your own idea.
- Keep private material out of public posts. Don't share private messages or name someone as a partner without their permission. Research contributions may be shared publicly with credit. We'll ask before listing your name or handle; personal health stories aren't research contributions.
- Help is voluntary. Nobody owes a contribution or a deadline. Partial work is welcome, and anyone can stop without explaining why.

Sudden new floaters, flashes, or a shadow or curtain over vision need a same-day eye exam.

## Reporting a problem

Message a moderator privately on Discord. A link to the post and a short explanation are enough to start. Please don't repost someone's private information in a public report.

Moderators can remove posts and use reminders, warnings, timeouts or bans, depending on what happened. If you're unsure whether something belongs here, ask a moderator.
