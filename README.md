# unfloat

An open, non-profit project working toward a real treatment for eye floaters: one that clears them and keeps the vitreous, the gel inside the eye.

## Where we are

We've gone through about 300 papers, the registered trials and public datasets. Now we're looking for surgeons and labs to work with. There are no lab results yet, and this will take years.

## How to help

👉 [Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/MN8XFEPuyJ)

Pick a small task, from 5 minutes to an evening. No science background needed, and no obligation.

If you're an eye surgeon or work in a lab, especially on collagen or the vitreous, we'd love to hear from you.

## How we work

Open by default: no patents, no company, nothing for sale. Contributors are credited in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md), and the [community rules](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) apply everywhere.

## Your eyes

Nothing here is medical advice. Sudden new floaters, flashes, or a shadow or curtain over your vision need a same-day eye exam.
