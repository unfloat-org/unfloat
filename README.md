# unfloat

I'm Daniil Morozov. I'm 26 and developed floaters a few months ago. They appeared fast, and now my vision looks like a fishing net. I want to find a treatment, for me and for everyone else living with this.

unfloat is an open effort toward a biological treatment for vitreous floaters. I don't know if we'll find one. I'd rather try than just accept that there's nothing we can do.

## The idea

The aim is to clear floaters selectively, by biological means, while keeping the vitreous, the gel inside the eye. That might mean helping the eye's own cells clear the floater material, using an injected agent such as an enzyme, or both.

Anything we try would need to leave the healthy gel and the rest of the eye unharmed. Liquefying or detaching the healthy vitreous would defeat the purpose. We're starting with biology, with no detour into developing a laser first.

## Where things stand

So far, I've been doing the groundwork: going through about 300 papers, the registered trials and public single-cell datasets. There are no lab results from unfloat yet.

I'm now looking for a surgeon who keeps floater tissue removed during surgery, and labs that could study it. We need to find out what makes that material different from healthy gel, then test whether it can be cleared without damaging the eye. A result showing that an idea doesn't work is worth sharing too.

This repository starts with the community basics. Research material will be added later, after review.

## How to help

If you work in eye surgery, a lab, collagen biology or the biology of the material around cells, I'd love to hear from you.

There's plenty to help with without a science background too. You could read a paper, translate a paragraph, or find a researcher who works on a question we're stuck on.

Join the Discord: https://discord.gg/MN8XFEPuyJ

Pick a small task that interests you. Help with whatever you can, when you can. There's no obligation to keep going, and partial results are welcome. You can also just follow along.

## How we work

The work is open by default. There are no patents on the project's outputs, no company, and I'm not selling anything. The goal is a treatment people can benefit from, with no profit motive.

We credit people for their contributions, using the name or handle they choose and with their OK. See [contributors](CONTRIBUTORS.md) and the [community rules](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Your eyes

Nothing here is medical advice. For questions about your own eyes or treatment, please ask your eye doctor. We aren't collecting health data, so please don't upload medical records or scans.

Sudden new floaters, flashes, or a shadow or curtain over vision need a same-day eye exam.

I can't promise a treatment or a date when one will be ready.
